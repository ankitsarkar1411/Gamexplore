🎮 GameXplore

GameXplore is an interactive mobile application built with Flutter and Firebase, designed to help sports enthusiasts explore, learn, and test their knowledge of popular sports. The app combines educational content, quizzes, and insights into the psychological, biological, and tactical aspects of sports.

✨ Features

🏏 Cricket, ⚽ Football, 🎾 Tennis – Explore multiple sports in one app.

📖 History – Learn about the origins and evolution of each sport.

❓ Quiz – Test your knowledge with engaging quizzes.

📋 Rules & Tactics – Understand gameplay strategies and official rules.

🧠 Psychological & Biological Aspects – Discover how sports impact the mind and body.

🩺 Injuries – Learn about common injuries and preventive measures.

🔥 Firebase Integration – Secure data storage, real-time updates, and user-friendly backend.

🛠 Tech Stack

Frontend: Flutter (Dart)

Backend & Database: Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage)

State Management: Provider / Riverpod (depending on your implementation)

Authentication: Firebase Auth (Email/Password, Google Sign-in)
